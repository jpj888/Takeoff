FAQs

Ask us anything
What ages do you coach?

We work with young players aged 5-17, grouped by age and ability so every athlete is challenged at the right level.

Does my child need experience?

No. Beginners through to representative players are welcome — sessions are pitched to each athlete's current level.

Where are sessions held?

Located in Sydney's inner west and eastern suburbs. Get in touch if you'd like to arrange a session closer to home.

Is strength training safe for kids?

Yes. Our athletic work is age-appropriate, focused on technique, bodyweight and movement quality rather than heavy loads.

What should my child bring?

Basketball shoes, comfortable training gear and a full water bottle.
